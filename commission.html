Questions

Good to know

How long does a commission take?

It depends on size and detail. A detailed piece can take around a week of sculpting, and you'll get a timeline with your quote.

What do I need to send?

Clear, well-lit photos of the subject from a few angles, plus any notes on pose, size and where the piece will be displayed.

Do you ship?

Yes. Pieces are packed carefully and delivery options are confirmed when you receive your quote.

How do I pay?

A deposit confirms the commission and the balance is due before delivery. Payment details are shared with your quote.
